# Community Code of Conduct

TandemVector Labs projects welcome constructive, evidence-based technical collaboration. All participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on verifiable behavior rather than personal characteristics, and keep criticism directed at technical decisions, not individuals.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, intimidation, unsolicited disclosure of private or confidential information, and deliberate disruption of project discussions are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate or remove abusive content, restrict participation, and escalate serious safety or security incidents. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and consistent with the applicable platform rules.

If you encounter conduct that requires confidential reporting, contact the authorized project maintainers privately rather than placing sensitive personal information in an issue. Repository-specific conduct policies take precedence.

These guidelines apply to project Issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions associated with this organization.
